# Getting started

cash-scout helps you **plan** a yearly budget, **project** your account balances forward to avoid overdrafts, and **control** what you really spent against the plan.

## The big picture

- Everything lives in a **book**: a separate set of finances, such as your private money or the household's. See [Books and members](02-books.md).
- Describe the book once in its **reference data**: accounts, payment types, and a tree of groups, categories and sub-categories. See [Reference data](03-reference-data.md).
- Record transactions, past and future, in the **journal**. See [Journal](04-journal.md).
- Make the year's **budget** ([Budget](06-budget.md)), then read the result in the **dashboard** ([Dashboard](07-dashboard.md)).

## A first session

1. Open **Books** from the menu (the ☰ button, top left) and create a book. Pick its currency with care: it cannot be changed later.
2. Open **Reference data** and add at least one account and one group with a category and a sub-category.
3. Open the **Journal** and add your first entry.

## Menu

The ☰ menu holds all the navigation: the sections of the open book, the list of books, the language, your account, and this help.
