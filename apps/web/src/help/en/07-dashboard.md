# Dashboard

The **Dashboard** reads one year at a glance. It never changes data, and every member can see it, viewers included. Amounts are in the book's currency.

## Reading it

- **Columns**: the 12 months and the year's total.
- **Rows**: your natures and balances, in the order set in the reference data. A nature opens on its groups, categories and sub-categories. Use _Expand all_ and _Collapse all_.
- A **scheduled** category's cell is the sum of the month's entries, reconciled or not. A **periodic** category's cell is what the month consumed so far, and its allocation for later months. Future values look different from past ones.
- **Unused budget** appears under an expanded periodic category, so that its sub-categories and this line add up to the category.
- **Round** hides the cents.
- **Account balances at month end** sums the entries up to each month's end. Future months are the projected balance. Virtual accounts are left out.

## Detail panel

Select a cell: the panel at the bottom lists the **entries** behind it, read-only. Resize or hide it, and open the journal from there.
